## Sales Follow-Up Tracker

A mobile-friendly, installable app for tracking leads, appointments, follow-ups, and your book of sales.

- Open `sales-tracker/index.html` on your phone, or host this repo (e.g. GitHub Pages) and visit `/sales-tracker/`.
- Use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option to install it like a native app — it works offline and stores all data privately on your device (no account, no server).
- Tabs: **Follow-Ups** (agenda of what's due), **Calendar** (month view with due-date dots), **Sales** (your book of sales with totals), and **Contacts** (full searchable list).
- Back up or move data anytime via Settings → Export/Import.
